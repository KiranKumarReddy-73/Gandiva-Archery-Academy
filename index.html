FAQS

Who can join the academy?
Anyone aged 6 and above is welcome to join! We train beginners and experienced archers alike.
What are the training timings?
Timings vary based on batches. Please contact us directly or visit the academy for the latest schedule.
Is equipment provided?
Yes, all essential archery equipment is provided during training sessions.
Do students get to participate in tournaments?
Absolutely! We encourage and train our students for state and national level tournaments every year.
Do you focus only on archery?
While archery is our core focus, we also emphasize physical fitness and discipline as key elements of the training.
Who is the coach?
Our sessions are led by Coach Dhananjay Tellaguti, in collaboration with the Andhra Pradesh Archery Academy.

History Of Our Academy

GANDIVA ARCHERY ACADEMY
Since 2015

Founded in the year 2015 in association with the Andhra Pradesh Archery Academy. Gandiva Archery Academy Tirupati began its journey with just a handful of passionate learners. Under the expert guidance of Coach Dhananjay Tellaguti, the academy steadily grew in strength and reputation. What started as a small initiative has now expanded to reach archery enthusiasts across the entire Tirupati district, becoming a recognized name for discipline, physical fitness, and tournament-level training.
